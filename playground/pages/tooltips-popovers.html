Tooltips & popovers

Bootstrap's .tooltip / .popover with IndigoKit's token treatment — near-black tooltip surface, white popover surface with a hairline border and small elevation. Initialized centrally by indigokit.js; Bootstrap/Popper handle all positioning and dismissal. Neither replaces essential labels: a tooltip is brief supplementary context, a popover is richer but still optional information.

Tooltips

Four placements. Triggers are hover + focus (Bootstrap's default) — keyboard users get the same information via the focus trigger.

Icon-only buttons

The button's accessible name comes from aria-label — the tooltip only adds supplementary context and is never the only name.

Keyboard: focus the buttons with Tab — the tooltip appears on focus, so hover-only information is never inaccessible.

Popovers

Richer contextual content — a title and a body. Click-triggered by default here; data-bs-content carries the body text (no hidden markup needed).

Dismissal behavior

Click-triggered popovers close on: clicking the trigger again, clicking outside, or pressing Escape — all stock Bootstrap behavior, no custom dismissal.

Edge-of-viewport behavior

Popper flips placement automatically near the edges — the trigger in the bottom corner opens its popover upward so nothing is cut off.

Accessibility notes

  • Tooltips and popovers are supplementary — they never replace the trigger's accessible name. Every trigger has real text or an aria-label.
  • Triggers use hover and focus (tooltips) or click/focus (popovers) — no hover-only information. Keyboard users tab to a trigger to read its tooltip.
  • Bootstrap 5.3 attaches tooltip/popover content to the trigger's aria-describedby when shown; no extra ARIA is added.
  • Dismissal is stock: click-away, trigger toggle, and Escape for popovers; blur for focus-triggered ones. No persistent overlays, no keyboard traps.
  • Icons in icon-only buttons are decorative (aria-hidden) with the aria-label carrying the name.
  • Popper flips placement near viewport edges, so content stays on-screen at every width.
