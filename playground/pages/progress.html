Accessibility notes

  • Every determinate bar uses role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, and an aria-label naming what is progressing.
  • Indeterminate progress (unknown duration) is intentionally not shown — Bootstrap has no built-in indeterminate bar, and inventing one is out of scope.
  • Semantic colors never carry meaning alone — the accompanying text states the value and its context, and the contrast fix keeps bar text readable (dark ink on amber/red/cyan, white on indigo/emerald).
  • The animated variant is Bootstrap's stock .progress-bar-animated; its prefers-reduced-motion: reduce block stops the animation for users who request reduced motion.
  • Labels live outside the bar where a narrow bar would crush the text — percentage + operation name remain readable at every width.
