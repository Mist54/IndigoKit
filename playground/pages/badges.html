Badges

Bootstrap's badge API with IndigoKit's token system — every badge below uses standard Bootstrap classes (.badge + .text-bg-*, optionally .rounded-pill) and Bootstrap positioning/spacing utilities. Badges are always paired with a .text-bg-* variant — a bare .badge has no visible background.

Semantic variants

Text colors come from Bootstrap's color-contrast() on the approved palette — dark text on emerald/cyan/amber/red/light, white on indigo/slate/dark.

Badges default to white-space: nowrap — for longer statuses, opt in to wrapping with Bootstrap's .text-wrap utility.

Contrast & accessibility

Every variant pair is AA-compliant (measured): white on indigo ≈ 7:1; black on slate ≈ 4.9:1; black on emerald ≈ 4.9:1; black on cyan ≈ 8.7:1; black on amber ≈ 9.4:1; black on red ≈ 5.6:1; black on light ≈ 19:1; white on dark ≈ 12:1. Badges are supplementary text — they add no ARIA of their own, and their text naturally joins the surrounding accessible name.
