Accessibility notes

  • A spinner alone is not an accessible loading message — every spinner has an accessible name: visible text, a .visually-hidden "Loading…" label, or an aria-label on role="status" (polite live region, announced when it appears).
  • In buttons, the spinner is aria-hidden and decorative — the label text ("Saving…", "Inviting…") communicates the state, and the button is disabled while in flight.
  • Colors come from the existing text-* utilities (currentcolor) — no custom spinner colors; contrast is the palette's established set.
  • Reduced motion: Bootstrap's built-in rule doubles the spinner animation speed under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce (1.5s) instead of stopping it — the state is still communicated by the text, never the motion alone.
  • The overlay demo is local to its card, adds no keyboard trap, and carries its own accessible label; a global loading manager is deliberately out of scope.
